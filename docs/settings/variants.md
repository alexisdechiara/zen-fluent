## How the choices combine

Two axes, so that choices combine: the **fill** (solid, standard, tint,
outline, subtle, transparent, translucent) and the **shape** (square,
rounded, pill). A **Pill** fill saved by 0.3 or before still reads as the
pill shape, and a **Ghost** fill saved by 0.4.1 or before as Subtle.

- The fill of a component is its own when it names one, else the one set
  for every component; Automatic on both keeps the look of the other
  settings (for instance **Button style** in the Sidebar group, which a
  buttons fill replaces).
- The shape is the component's shape, else the shape set for every
  component.
  Rounded keeps the component's own radius (and blocks a shape set for every
  component).

## Fills

The names are Microsoft's: Fluent 2's button appearances (outline, subtle,
transparent), its badge appearances (tint) and WinUI's fill colors (control
fill, control solid fill, subtle fill). Sources:
[Fluent UI Button](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui/blob/master/packages/react-components/react-button/library/src/components/Button/Button.types.ts),
[Fluent UI Badge](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui/blob/master/packages/react-components/react-badge/library/src/components/Badge/Badge.types.ts),
[WinUI theme resources](https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/blob/main/controls/dev/CommonStyles/Common_themeresources_any.xaml).

- **Solid**: opaque (WinUI's `ControlSolidFillColorDefault`: white in
  light, dark grey in dark) with the elevation stroke; nothing shows
  through. In the Edge style, with Fluent 2's hairline.
- **Standard**: WinUI's default button: the see-through control fill
  (`ControlFillColorDefault`) and the elevation stroke (the Edge style: the
  address bar's white field and hairline). Fluent 2 calls it the secondary
  button.
- **Tint**: half the control fill inside the outline's 1 px stroke, the full
  control fill on hover (Fluent 2's tint badge).
- **Outline**: a 1 px stroke, the subtle fills on hover and press. Contrast
  themes draw it in the button text color.
- **Subtle**: no fill at rest, the subtle fill (`SubtleFillColorSecondary`)
  on hover (Fluent 2's subtle button, WinUI's command bar buttons).
- **Transparent**: no fill, even on hover; glyphs turn to the accent on
  hover (Fluent 2's transparent button).
- **Translucent**: Zen's own see-through fills, a share of the text color
  that lets the window color (and the space's gradient) show; the selected
  item takes Zen's white veil. Not a Microsoft name: Zen's look.

## Components

Buttons take the fill at rest, on hover and pressed. Lists (tabs, pinned
tabs, spaces) take it on their selected item: the others stay bare, with the
fill's hover. Solid raises the selected item on an opaque fill, Standard on
the control fill, Tint gives it the control fill in the outline, Outline
rings it, Subtle gives it the subtle fill, Transparent leaves it bare (the
accent pill or dot still marks it), Translucent lays Zen's white veil on it.

- **Toolbar and sidebar buttons**: the sidebar's top and foot buttons (menu,
  compact mode, back, forward, reload, Library, New) and the toolbar's with
  multiple toolbars. The new tab row, the media card and the spaces keep their
  own look. Pill on these square buttons draws circles.
- **Extension buttons** and **Extensions row**: the buttons of the row of
  pinned extensions (Extensions group), and the row itself, a surface 4 px
  around them. Both apply at once; the row's Automatic leaves it to **Row
  background** (Extensions group) and does not follow the fill set for
  every component. A **Fill and shape for: The whole row** choice saved by
  0.4.2 or before still moves the buttons' fill and shape to the row, until
  a row fill or shape is picked.
- **Essentials**: the fill is for the tiles at rest; the selected tile keeps
  **Selected Essential** (Sidebar group). The shape is for every tile.
  Subtle and Transparent leave bare icons at rest, like the taskbar.
- **Tabs**: the selected tab keeps its accent pill. Pill rounds the row ends.
- **Pinned tabs**: the pinned tabs of a space, the tabs of its folders
  included.
- **Folder headers**: the folder's label row, as a button.
- **Space buttons**: the current space takes the fill. A fill replaces
  **Workspace icon style**'s Fill; its Pill dot and Minimal fading stay.
- **URL bar**: the field at rest and hovered; focused or open, it keeps its
  look. Solid is an opaque field, Standard the style's own field (both
  bring a field back with the Transparent URL bar shape), Tint a light fill
  in a stroke, Outline a stroke without fill, Translucent Zen's see-through
  field. Subtle and Transparent keep a faint stroke: without any, nothing
  would show where to type. The shape only rounds the field: **URL bar shape**'s Pill
  also turns its buttons into circles.
- **URL bar buttons**: site information, permissions, tracking protection,
  bookmark star and page actions.
- **Badges**: extension counts and the Library's downloads count. Solid and
  Standard are the accent, Tint an accent tint in an accent ring, Subtle and
  Transparent accent text on the window color, Outline adds an accent ring, Translucent a see-through
  accent. Square gives them 2 px corners.
- **Panel and dialog buttons**: the standard buttons of the flyouts
  (downloads, site information) and of the dialogs; the accent button keeps
  its fill but takes the shape.

The window's caption buttons (minimize, maximize, close) follow the
**Caption buttons** setting: a fill or a shape would no longer look like
Windows'.
